Every name on my list is someone I saved. And not one of them came back.

I climbed out of the tunnel. The man from the radio was waiting by the trucks. He handed me a badge. My face. My name. City Grid Control, night supervisor.

And I remembered. On day one, something got into the grid. It moved through the wires, house to house, wherever a light was on. And the people inside stopped being people.

So we turned the city off. To starve it.

Then a new line appeared in my notebook: "Don't trust him. Look at his eyes."
