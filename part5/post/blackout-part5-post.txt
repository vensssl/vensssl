My phone had been dead for seven days. Tonight, she plugged it in.

The last video on it was from day one. 9:14 PM. A rooftop. The whole skyline still lit.

Then the lights started going out. Block by block. Like someone was checking names off a list.

When the last tower went dark, the camera flipped around. Six people in gray coats. One of them was her.

And the one holding the phone was me.

"You were never on their list, Mark. You wrote it."
