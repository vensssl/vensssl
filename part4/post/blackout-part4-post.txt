DESCRIPTION:
Seven days ago, Chicago went dark. Every door got a red X. Except mine.

Last night, a voice under the street said my name and told me one thing: "Don't get in the trucks."

So we climbed down.

Under the city there are dozens of people hiding. And a woman in a gray coat who says I'm on their list because of what I saw on day one.

But I didn't see anything on day one.

Then I opened my notebook. The first page isn't in my handwriting.
